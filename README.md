2. Cybersecurity Incident: French Supermarket Cyberattack (October 2026):
Reports described a cyberattack affecting internet-connected refrigeration systems at supermarkets in France. The reported incident involved hackers remotely changing refrigeration temperatures, potentially causing food spoilage and disrupting store operations.

Why It Matters?
